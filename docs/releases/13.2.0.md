# 13.2.0 — Evidence-backed outcome acceptance

- Derives the original goal, representative scenarios and observable completion criteria before implementation, analysis or review; verifies facts before conclusions, investigates accessible unknowns, and keeps final claims within their evidence coverage.
- Selects proportional functional, UX/UI, accessibility, performance, security and operational evidence from the changed surface and risk, including realistic startup/loading, empty or failure, data-scale and narrow-screen states where applicable.
- Adds selectable incremental and one-time sweep verification cadence. Incremental work keeps the agreed functional baseline and validates coherent increments. A sweep may defer routine per-feature checks in an isolated checkout, while risk-needed architecture, integration-feasibility and consequential-assumption checks happen before dependent work. Both cadences use the same final checks, realistic-state audits, independent whole-outcome review and repair of relevant shortcomings that prevent the accepted outcome.
- Keeps unknown or inaccessible evidence and material limitations visible, separates quality completion from draft delivery, and preserves existing authority boundaries.

Instruction-only changes; no runtime migration is needed.
