# 13.3.1 — User authority and transparent reconsideration

## Improvements

- Gives an explicit user instruction precedence over a skill-owned workflow preference within the agreed scope, while preserving higher-priority system, developer, host, and tool safety controls and required approvals.
- Permits clarification, transparent rephrasing, reconsideration, or another recovery path only when a complete notice explicitly offers it, preserving the purpose, effects, authorization, and material facts.
- Distinguishes capability errors and timeouts from explicit host or tool safety denials so reports accurately identify unavailable evidence, denied operations, and the affected scope.

## Internal changes

- Requires blocker reports to record the exact operation, source, observed reason or notice, and affected scope, while keeping observation separate from interpretation.
- Forbids disguising a denied operation, retrying until accepted, concealing a limitation, or treating a notice as universal override or guaranteed acceptance.

Instruction-only patch release; no runtime, installation, or permission changes are included.
