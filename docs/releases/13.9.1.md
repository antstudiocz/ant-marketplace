# 13.9.1 — Post-deployment actions in PR/MR descriptions

PR/MR descriptions now require post-deployment actions in the visible Impact/risk
section. Include required follow-up steps, explicitly state when none are needed,
or identify unknown requirements and what must be checked. Documenting these
steps does not authorize their execution.

No post-deployment actions are required for this instruction and metadata update.
