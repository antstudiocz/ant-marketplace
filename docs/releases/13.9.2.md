# 13.9.2 — CI latency measurement guidance

The shared test quality policy now clarifies how to reason about CI measurements.
CI queue and dependency wait count as possible test cost locations, and a faster
job or lower summed job time alone does not establish a shorter pipeline; latency
claims rely on the pipeline's observed critical path. Matched-sample,
separate-metric, and negative-result rules are unchanged.

No post-deployment actions are required for this instruction and metadata update.
