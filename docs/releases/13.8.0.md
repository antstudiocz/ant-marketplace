# 13.8.0 — Evidence-grounded independent review

Adds a provider-aware overall review of the full outcome, with specialists chosen
only when the change needs focused expertise. Findings are dispositioned as
confirmed, refuted, or unverified using evidence, and final-flow checks are tied
to the changed behavior and candidate.

The bounded read-only Claude review route was verified. A full reciprocal
cross-provider end-to-end review was not performed, and no empirical improvement
in finding detection has been measured.
